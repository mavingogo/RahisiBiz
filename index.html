Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use my existing WhatsApp Business number?

Yes! You can link your existing phone number directly by scanning the QR code, or you can register an official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API number through our dashboard.

Will my WhatsApp number get banned?

No. RahisiBiz uses human-like response pacing, obeys WhatsApp anti-spam policies, and handles opt-in inquiries strictly in compliance with Meta's terms of service.

Can my human team take over the chat whenever they want?

Absolutely. Whenever you send a message from your phone or web inbox, the AI automatically pauses on that contact for 30 minutes (or until you re-enable it).
